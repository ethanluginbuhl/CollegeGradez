# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making participation in CollegeGradez respectful,
constructive, and welcoming. Participants should assume good faith, give
actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather than
people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publishing private information
without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or limit
participation when needed to protect the community. Report concerns privately
through the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue.
